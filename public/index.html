PhishScore.ai is a community scam intelligence dashboard. Paste any suspicious email, text message, phone number, or link to get an instant AI-powered risk score, scam category, and red-flag breakdown — for free.

What you can do here

  • Quick Scan: get an instant risk score for an email, message, phone number, or link.
  • Analyze Threat: run a deeper AI investigation into a suspected scam.
  • PhishScore Radar: view live, community-reported scam intelligence and trending threats.
  • Threat Glossary: browse definitions and red flags for common scam categories.
